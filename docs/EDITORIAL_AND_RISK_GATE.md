# Dasha News — Editorial Automation & Risk Gate

## Principle

Maximize interesting, locally relevant, useful news while keeping factual traceability, attribution, originality, and platform/legal risk under control.

This is an automated newsroom, not a system that blindly rewrites whatever it finds.

## Evidence taxonomy

Use:
- fact
- official
- claim
- allegation
- disputed
- unverified

Never silently upgrade an allegation or claim into a fact.

## Story handling

For each story:
- identify the underlying event;
- cluster duplicate reports;
- preserve independent sources;
- extract named entities, location, time, figures, quotes, and claims;
- compare conflicting details;
- record what is confirmed and what is not;
- generate an original concise summary;
- attach source attribution;
- retain update history.

## Risk gates

Automatic publication is acceptable for ordinary, well-supported stories that pass validation.

Raise the threshold or hold when a story involves:
- serious criminal allegations against identifiable people
- unverified accusations
- communal/religious tension
- deaths or casualty numbers with conflicting reports
- medical/public-health claims
- manipulated or synthetic media
- election-related claims
- defamation-sensitive allegations
- private/sensitive personal information
- unclear image provenance

A hold must not stop the rest of the newsroom.

## Headlines

Headlines may be concise, energetic, curiosity-driven, and locally relevant. They must not:
- invent facts;
- imply guilt where only an allegation exists;
- manufacture quotes;
- conceal material uncertainty;
- use fake urgency;
- claim an image is real when it is illustrative.

## Political coverage

Dasha may cover politics extensively. The system must distinguish:
- documented event/action;
- direct statement;
- allegation;
- analysis/opinion attributed to its source.

Do not infer motives as facts.

## Originality

Dasha must add value through:
- fact normalization;
- source comparison;
- local context;
- clustering;
- corrections;
- concise original writing;
- language adaptation;
- personalization;
- audio/short presentation.

Do not merely synonym-swap source articles.

## Corrections

If a published story becomes materially wrong:
- update the canonical fact record;
- show a correction/update state;
- preserve audit history;
- update the user-facing story;
- avoid silently rewriting history where the change matters.

## Human review philosophy

The owner is not a newsroom employee. Human review is a safety valve for exceptional stories, not a daily operating requirement. The default path should remain autonomous.
